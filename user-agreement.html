User Agreement

Effective 11 October 2026

These terms cover your use of Vojygaj, an app for making rose-themed greeting cards on your own device.

The app

You choose a template or a blank canvas, add a photo, place stickers, write or dictate a greeting, and confirm a render. A successful render saves one PNG in the app and uses one Card Credit. Previewing a draft does not use a credit. Saving the same sealed PNG to Photos again does not use another credit. A later render creates a new edition and does not replace the earlier PNG.

One-Tap Login

You open the app with One-Tap Login after you agree to the Privacy Policy and this User Agreement. The session stays on this device. There is no password, no Sign in with Apple, and no cloud library. Log Out ends the session and leaves your cards and Card Credits in place. Delete Account removes the session and the cards, photos, drafts, and Card Credits stored in the app. Your cards exist only in this installation until you delete them, clear all data, delete the account, or delete the app.

Card Credits

Card Credits are consumable items sold by Apple. Prices and names shown at purchase are Apple’s. A small starter amount may be placed on the device when you begin, and again after you clear data and begin once more. That starter amount is not an App Store product. Credits cannot be restored, transferred, or redeemed for cash. Refunds are handled by Apple. Clearing all data deletes the remaining balance on the device.

Your content

You are responsible for the photos and words you put on a card. Do not use the app to infringe someone else’s rights.

Availability

Rendering depends on free space on the device. If a render is cancelled or the file cannot be saved, the draft remains and no Card Credit is used for that attempt.
