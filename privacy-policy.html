Privacy Policy

Effective 11 October 2026

Vojygaj makes greeting cards on your device. This policy describes what the app handles. One-Tap Login stores a session only on this device. There is no password and no Sign in with Apple. The app does not upload your photos or card files to a server we run.

Data on this device

Drafts, templates you choose, sticker positions, greeting text, photo copies, sealed PNG cards, and the Card Credit ledger are stored in the app’s private container using on-device storage. Preferences such as whether you have passed the first screen are stored in on-device settings. Deleting the app, or using Clear All Data, removes this information from the device, including any remaining Card Credits.

Camera

The camera is used only after you tap Take Photo, to capture a still picture for the card. The app does not record video and does not request the microphone for that photo.

Photos

Photo library access is requested when you tap Import from Photos, so you can choose a picture. A copy is kept in the app. The original in your library is not edited. Add-photo access is requested separately when you tap Save to Photos, to write the finished PNG into your library.

Microphone and speech

The microphone and speech recognition are requested only after you tap Dictate. Spoken words are placed in a preview. They are written into the greeting only if you tap Insert into Message. You can type the greeting instead. Recognition is requested on the device when the system allows it.

Purchases

Card Credits are consumable in-app purchases processed by Apple. The app records a verified transaction identifier on this device so the same transaction is not granted twice. Credits are not restored after you remove the app or clear its data. Apple handles payment and refunds. If Apple revokes a purchase, the app removes only the unused part of that grant. Cards already rendered stay in the app.

Log out and delete account

Log Out ends the session on this device and returns you to One-Tap Login. Your cards, photos, and Card Credits stay. Delete Account removes that local session together with drafts, photos, sealed cards, and the Card Credit ledger stored in the app. Copies already saved to Photos stay in Photos. Apple is not asked to refund purchases.

What we do not do

The app does not offer a password, does not offer Sign in with Apple, and does not sell your photos. It does not track you across other companies’ apps. It does not request tracking permission or push notifications.
